Why this screen is empty

CLV identifies an edge in a few hundred bets instead of several thousand. It needs two prices per bet: the number you took, and the de-vigged closing number.

Courvix shows the running mean including when it's negative. Nothing appears here without real data behind it, because a track record you can't audit is exactly what every competitor is selling.
